Thoroughly explore the Alafya platform before creating the video. Understand its functionality and capture its most important features.

Then showcase the Nasser Family Platform as a more sophisticated, modern, visually impressive, and premium digital family experience.

IMPORTANT:
- Use real platform screenshots and actual interface elements.
- Do not invent features or fictional pages.
- Do not simply copy the Alafya design.
- Build a distinctive visual identity for the Nasser Family Platform.
- The advertisement must showcase the platform in depth, not just display its homepage or family tree.
